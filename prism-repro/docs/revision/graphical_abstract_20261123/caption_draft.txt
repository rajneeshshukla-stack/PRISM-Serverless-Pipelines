Under a new spike, PRISM violated 2.62% of 15-minute windows (95% interval +/-1.50), against 4.02% (+/-2.67) for smoothed headroom and 7.62% (+/-2.31) for unsmoothed headroom, at a weekly cost of USD 1,241, against 1,157 and 1,082: both headroom rules were cheaper, and PRISM did not meet the 2% target on average (it met the target on 1 of 5 traces). When the default 1024 MB cannot meet the deadline, PRISM selects a larger memory size if its risk budget is small enough (2048 MB or more in every epoch at eps 0.02); a fixed 2048 MB headroom rule met the deadline at 13-27% lower cost in the regimes tested (two traces, post hoc, no caps), and adaptation to changes in execution characteristics within a run was not tested.
